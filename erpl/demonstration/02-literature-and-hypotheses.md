# ERPL Demonstration — 02 | Literature and hypotheses

## Input

The distinction between manuscript quality, scientific novelty and independence identified in the first stage.

## Research response

Two published cases provide useful contrasting evidence:

- Sakana AI's AI Scientist-v2 workshop experiment evaluates whether machine-generated manuscripts can receive favorable scientific reviews.
- Google's 2025 AI co-scientist research investigates hypothesis generation and empirical evaluation in biomedical discovery.

The cases were selected for their different evidence types, not as representative samples of the entire industry or the latest releases of either system.

### Competing propositions

**H1:** Favorable peer review of an AI-generated manuscript is sufficient to establish independent scientific discovery.

**H2:** Favorable peer review provides evidence of manuscript-level scientific quality, but independent discovery also requires evidence about novelty, the origin of the finding, and its validation.

H1 would be weakened by a case where favorable reviews occur without sufficient information to establish novelty or independent discovery. H2 would be strengthened if distinct experimental or provenance evidence is required to resolve those questions.
