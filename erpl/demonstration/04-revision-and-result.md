# ERPL Demonstration — 04 | Revision and research result

## Revision

The initial framing implicitly treated discovery as a positive, previously unknown finding. The evidence requires a broader definition of research contribution: a defensible negative result or independent rediscovery can also have scientific value, although its novelty and priority claims differ.

The research question is refined to ask what evidence is sufficient for **each claimed type of scientific contribution**, rather than treating peer review as a universal substitute for validation.

## Final research result

**Peer-review success is relevant evidence of manuscript-level scientific merit, but it is not sufficient on its own to establish independent scientific discovery.**

A defensible assessment of AI-generated research should distinguish:

| Claim | Evidence needed |
|---|---|
| Research output quality | Evaluation of the manuscript, methods and reporting |
| Empirical validity | Appropriate data, experiments and reproducible analyses |
| Novel contribution | Comparison against relevant prior knowledge |
| Independent discovery | Documented origin of the finding and the role of human intervention |
| Research value | A supported positive finding, a meaningful negative result, or another justified contribution |

This distinction prevents research-production systems from optimizing solely for favorable manuscript evaluations while neglecting the actual scientific value of the underlying work.

## Deliverable and limitations

The result is a source-based analytical report with a refined evaluation framework. It answers the assigned question, identifies what the selected cases establish, and states what they cannot establish.

This is a limited demonstration of human-directed AI research activity, not a systematic literature review, an independent reproduction of the cited experiments, or proof that ERPL outperforms other research systems. The next research step, if warranted, would be a prospectively designed comparative study with blinded assessment of actual research outcomes.

**Demonstration status:** research report delivered. No public research service is offered through this example.
