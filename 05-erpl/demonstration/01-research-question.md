# ERPL Demonstration — 01 | Research question

## Input

Does peer-review success alone establish that an AI research system has independently discovered new scientific knowledge?

## Research response

The question concerns the relationship between three different outcomes: manuscript quality, scientific novelty and independence of the discovery process. These must be evaluated separately.

A research system could produce a credible manuscript containing a negative result, propose a previously known explanation, or develop an original hypothesis that has not been experimentally validated. These outcomes are not equivalent.

The research object is therefore the **evidentiary relationship between a research output and the underlying discovery process**.

## Scope and deliverable

Investigate selected documented AI-for-science results, determine what each result establishes, and develop a defensible distinction between manuscript evaluation, discovery and independent validation. This is a bounded documentary analysis, not a new laboratory experiment.

**Next research action:** examine primary publications and develop competing propositions.
